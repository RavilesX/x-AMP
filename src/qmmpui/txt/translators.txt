The Spanish translation began as the one Qmmp carries, by the three
translators named above, and is kept up with the strings x-AMP adds.

x-AMP shipped translations for thirty languages while it followed Qmmp
directly. Those it no longer carries were frozen at the revision this fork
was taken from and drifted further with every string added here, so they
were removed rather than left to decay. Everyone who wrote them is credited
in the AUTHORS file, which keeps the upstream record in full.

Translations are welcome: https://github.com/RavilesX/x-AMP
